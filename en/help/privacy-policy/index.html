Privacy Policy

By using the Huan app, you are trusting us with your information. We understand this is a significant responsibility, which is why we are committed to protecting your data and putting you in control. This Privacy Policy is intended to help you understand what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can update, manage, export, and delete your information.

1. Information Collected

The operator of this app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
All data is stored only on the user's local device and will not leave the user's computer.

4. Third-Party Sharing and Disclosure

  • Personal information is not shared with, transferred to, or publicly disclosed to any third parties under any circumstances.
  • No cross-border data transfers occur.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • All data is stored on the local disk, and the retention period is entirely at the user's discretion.
  • Users may also manually delete log data.
  • Upon uninstallation of the app, huan-app/config/* will be automatically deleted, whereas %APPDATA%/Local/io.github.huan-app/* must be deleted manually.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is intended exclusively for adults.
If you are a minor, please use this app under the supervision of a guardian and ensure that your guardian has read this policy.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be revised in conjunction with app version updates. The revised policy is distributed along with the software installation package, and no separate push notifications will be sent.
